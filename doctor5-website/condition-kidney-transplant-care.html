Kidney Transplant Care & Graft Longevity

A reassuring guide to living with a transplanted kidney, adhering to medications, and protecting your gift of life.

The Gift of Renal Transplantation

Receiving a kidney transplant offers remarkable liberation from dialysis, providing normal renal filtration, natural erythropoietin production, and unrestricted dietary flexibility. However, because the donor kidney is recognized by your immune system as foreign tissue, maintaining graft health is a lifelong, active partnership between you and your nephrologist.

Three Golden Rules of Transplant Medication:

1. Never Skip a Dose: Immunosuppressants (Tacrolimus/Cyclosporine, Mycophenolate, Prednisolone) must be taken at the exact same hour every day.
2. Avoid Drug Interactions: Never start any antibiotic, antifungal, herbal remedy, or OTC painkiller without prior approval from your nephrologist.
3. Attend Periodic Lab Checks: Monthly trough drug level checks ensure you receive enough protection against rejection without injuring your kidneys.

Recognizing Warning Signs of Rejection or Infection

Contact Dr. Aishwarya Lal immediately if you notice any of the following symptoms:

  • Fever or Chills: Even a mild temperature (above 38°C / 100.4°F) requires urgent assessment.
  • Sudden Drop in Urine Output: Or rapid swelling in legs and sudden weight gain.
  • Tenderness Over the Graft Site: Pain, tenderness, or swelling in the lower abdomen where the new kidney is placed.
  • Unexplained Fatigue or Flu-Like Malaise: Often accompanied by elevated serum creatinine on routine checks.

Long-Term Health Maintenance

Post-transplant longevity depends on whole-body wellness. Dr. Aishwarya monitors cardiovascular health, screens for CMV and BK viruses, manages post-transplant diabetes (NODAT), and enforces strict sun protection and cancer screening protocols.
