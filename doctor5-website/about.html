About Dr. Aishwarya Lal

Consultant Nephrologist, Kidney Specialist & Renal Transplant Consultant

Philosophy of Care

Listening attentively, treating holistically.

Dr. Aishwarya Lal is a senior consultant nephrologist dedicated to providing thoughtful, science-backed, and empathetic kidney care. With over 15 years in clinical practice, she has guided more than 10,000 patients through challenging renal conditions ranging from early asymptomatic proteinuria to end-stage kidney disease and successful organ transplantation.

Her clinical philosophy centers on communication. Dr. Aishwarya believes that patients and their families should never leave a consultation confused or frightened by medical terms. She takes the time to break down complex renal pathology—explaining creatinine levels, glomerular filtration rates (eGFR), and urine protein leaks in clear, reassuring everyday language.

Rather than relying solely on pharmaceuticals, she formulates practical, sustainable lifestyle and nutritional blueprints that help patients protect their kidneys without compromising their daily quality of life.
