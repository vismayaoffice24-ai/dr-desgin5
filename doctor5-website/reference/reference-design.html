Frequently asked questions

When should I see a kidney specialist?

If tests show a change in kidney function, or you have swelling, urine changes, or long-standing blood pressure or diabetes.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Previous reports, a list of your medicines and any questions you have.

Does high creatinine always mean kidney disease?

Not always. It is read together with your history and other tests.

How can I protect my kidneys every day?

Control blood pressure and sugar, drink enough water, avoid unnecessary painkillers and get regular check-ups.
