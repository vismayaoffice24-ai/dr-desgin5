Privacy & Medical Confidentiality Policy

Our commitment to patient confidentiality, data protection, and transparent medical ethics.

Patient Confidentiality & Ethical Standards

At Dr. Aishwarya Lal Kidney Care Clinic, we adhere to the highest international and national standards of medical ethics and patient privacy. When you share personal information, medical history, lab reports, or contact details with us—whether through an in-person consultation, telephone inquiry, or WhatsApp consultation—your information is treated with strict clinical confidentiality.

1. Information We Collect

To provide safe, accurate medical advice and manage appointment bookings, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Full name, telephone/mobile number, email address, and residential city.
  • Clinical Information: Medical history, previous diagnoses, current prescription medications, and submitted blood/urine test results.
  • Appointment Preferences: Preferred dates, consultation mode (in-person vs. remote review), and chief medical complaints.

2. How Your Information is Used

Your information is used exclusively for:

  • Scheduling, confirming, and managing your medical consultations.
  • Providing personalized nephrology evaluations and diagnostic guidance.
  • Communicating critical appointment updates or lab review conclusions via telephone or secure WhatsApp chat.

We never sell, rent, lease, or share your personal contact or medical information with third-party marketers, insurance brokers, or external commercial organizations under any circumstances.

3. Medical Disclaimer & Emergency Situations

Non-Emergency Notice:

Website content and online WhatsApp communications are intended for non-emergency scheduling, educational health literacy, and elective report reviews. If you are experiencing sudden acute symptoms—such as chest pain, severe shortness of breath, acute confusion, or total inability to urinate—please visit the nearest hospital emergency department or call your local emergency ambulance service immediately.

4. Questions & Contact

If you have any questions regarding how your health data is handled or wish to update your contact preferences, please reach out to our administrative desk at vismaya@stackertec.com or call +91 97458 66333.
