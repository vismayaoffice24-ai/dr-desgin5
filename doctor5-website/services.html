What to Expect

Our 4-Step Consultation Process

We believe in clear steps so you always know what comes next.

1

Detailed Review

Dr. Aishwarya thoroughly reviews your medical history, current medications, blood creatinine, and urine tests.

2

Plain Explanation

You receive a clear, jargon-free explanation of what your kidney numbers mean and what stage you are at.

3
