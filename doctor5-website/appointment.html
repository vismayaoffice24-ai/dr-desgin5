What to Bring to Your Visit

  • Recent blood test reports (serum creatinine, urea, electrolytes).
  • Urine routine and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (uACR).
  • Ultrasound abdomen or CT kidney scans.
  • List of all current medications with dosages.
  • Previous discharge summaries if hospitalized.
