Transplant Follow-Up & Graft Protection

Comprehensive pre-transplant workup, immunosuppression drug optimization, and lifelong graft preservation.

Lifelong Partnership in Transplant Success

A kidney transplant represents the gold standard therapy for advanced chronic kidney disease, providing patients with independence from dialysis machines and vastly superior life expectancy. However, receiving a donor kidney is only the first step—long-term graft longevity demands rigorous, specialized nephrological surveillance.

Dr. Aishwarya Lal has managed hundreds of kidney transplant recipients over her 15-year career. She provides vigilant post-transplant monitoring to maintain optimal immunosuppression, prevent both acute and chronic rejection, and treat secondary metabolic concerns.

Key Pillars of Post-Transplant Management:

• Therapeutic Drug Monitoring (TDM): Precise dosing of Tacrolimus, Cyclosporine, and Mycophenolate (MMF) to avoid both under-immunosuppression (rejection risk) and over-immunosuppression (toxicity & infection risk).
• Infection Vigilance: Screening and prophylaxis for Cytomegalovirus (CMV), BK virus nephropathy, and fungal/bacterial infections.
• Cardiometabolic Health: Managing new-onset diabetes after transplantation (NODAT), hyperlipidemia, and hypertension.

Pre-Transplant Comprehensive Workup

For patients preparing for kidney transplantation, Dr. Aishwarya conducts:

  • Recipient Medical Clearance: Cardiac evaluation, infectious disease serologies, dental clearance, and malignancy screening.
  • Immunological Compatibility Assessment: HLA tissue typing, panel reactive antibodies (PRA), and donor-specific cross-matching.
  • Living Donor Medical Evaluation: Ensuring absolute donor safety, split renal function confirmation via DTPA/DMSA scan, and ethical compliance.
