Preventive Kidney Health & Teleconsultation

Comprehensive report audits, annual preventive checkups, second opinions, and remote follow-up consultations.

Proactive Prevention Over Crisis Management

Kidneys work silently in the background, filtering approximately 180 liters of blood each day, balancing vital electrolytes, maintaining acid-base harmony, and activating bone-strengthening Vitamin D. Because early renal deterioration causes no pain, proactive preventative evaluation is the single most powerful defense against chronic kidney disease.

Dr. Aishwarya Lal offers structured annual preventative renal assessments and dedicated remote review services for patients seeking clarity on abnormal lab tests or desiring an expert second opinion.

Who Needs an Annual Preventative Kidney Check?

• Individuals over 40 years of age with diabetes or hypertension.
• Anyone with a family history of kidney failure or polycystic kidney disease.
• Patients taking long-term medications, pain relievers, or acid reflux drugs.
• Individuals with a history of recurrent kidney stones or single functional kidney.

What is Included in Our Preventive Assessment

  • Serum Creatinine & eGFR Audit: Precise renal function evaluation factoring age, weight, and muscle mass.
  • Urine Spot Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio (uACR): Detecting minute microgram leaks of protein before blood tests show changes.
  • Electrolyte & Mineral Panel: Sodium, potassium, bicarbonate, calcium, and uric acid checks.
  • Medication Safety Audit: Reviewing all prescription drugs and over-the-counter supplements to eliminate nephrotoxic risks.
  • Kidney-Protective Diet Coaching: Clarifying daily fluid goals, sodium reduction strategies, and practical protein balance.

Convenient WhatsApp & Video Consultations

If you live outside Kochi, are unable to travel due to mobility limitations, or simply need an expert review of your recent hospital discharge summary or lab report, Dr. Aishwarya Lal provides structured remote consultations via WhatsApp and secure video link.
