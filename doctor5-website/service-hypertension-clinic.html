Hypertension & Kidney Clinic

Expert diagnostic workup for secondary hypertension, resistant high blood pressure, and renal vascular disease.

The Intimate Kidney-Blood Pressure Link

Kidneys are the primary regulators of long-term blood pressure in the human body through fluid balance, sodium excretion, and renin hormone production. When blood pressure remains persistently elevated, it stiffens and narrows the fine renal arterioles, progressively suffocating filtering nephrons.

Conversely, diseased kidneys release excess renin and retain excessive sodium and water, driving blood pressure dangerously high. At Dr. Aishwarya Lal's clinic, we break this destructive cycle.

What is Resistant Hypertension?

Blood pressure that remains persistently above 130/80 mmHg despite taking three different antihypertensive medications at optimal doses (one of which is a diuretic). In such cases, underlying kidney disorders or renal artery stenosis are frequent culprits requiring nephrological investigation.

Comprehensive Hypertension Diagnostic Services

  • Secondary Hypertension Workup: Screening for renal artery stenosis via Renal Doppler Ultrasound, primary hyperaldosteronism, and pheochromocytoma.
  • 24-Hour Ambulatory Blood Pressure Monitoring (ABPM): Analyzing circadian nocturnal dipping patterns (loss of night-time BP dip is a major marker of renal strain).
  • Target Organ Damage Assessment: Evaluating microalbuminuria, left ventricular hypertrophy on ECG/Echo, and fundoscopic retinal vascular changes.
  • Rational Drug Regimen Design: Avoiding duplicate or counter-productive medications while prioritizing kidney-protective ACEi/ARBs, calcium channel blockers, and mineralocorticoid receptor antagonists.
