Early Detection Matters

When should you visit a nephrologist?

Persistent swelling of the feet or face, frothy or tea-colored urine, rising serum creatinine, long-standing diabetes, uncontrolled high blood pressure, or recurrent kidney stones are all important reasons for a specialist consultation.
