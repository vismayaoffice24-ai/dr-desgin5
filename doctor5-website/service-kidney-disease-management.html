Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Clinic

Early diagnosis, staging, and personalized slowing therapies for long-term renal preservation.

Understanding Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) refers to a gradual, progressive loss of renal filtration capacity occurring over months or years. Because kidneys have substantial reserve capacity, individuals can lose up to 70% of kidney function without experiencing overt symptoms. This makes regular screening and timely nephrological intervention vital.

At Dr. Aishwarya Lal's CKD clinic, our primary objective is to arrest or substantially delay the progression of kidney damage. With contemporary medical strategies, disciplined blood pressure and sugar control, and tailored nutrition, patients at stages 1, 2, 3, and even 4 can maintain stable kidney function for many years—often avoiding dialysis entirely.

Staging of Chronic Kidney Disease (Based on eGFR):

• Stage 1 (eGFR 90+): Kidney damage with normal filtration.
• Stage 2 (eGFR 60–89): Mild decline in kidney function.
• Stage 3 (eGFR 30–59): Moderate decline (split into 3a and 3b). Crucial window for intervention.
• Stage 4 (eGFR 15–29): Severe reduction. Pre-dialysis & vascular access planning.
• Stage 5 (eGFR <15): Kidney failure requiring dialysis or transplantation.

Comprehensive Clinical Evaluation

When you visit Dr. Aishwarya Lal for CKD management, your consultation includes:

  • Serum Creatinine & eGFR Verification: Calculating kidney filtration accurately using age, gender, and validated CKD-EPI equations.
  • Urine Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio (uACR): Quantifying the degree of microscopic protein leak, which is the strongest predictor of progression.
  • Electrolyte & Acid-Base Audit: Checking potassium, sodium, bicarbonate, calcium, and phosphorus balances.
  • Kidney Ultrasound Review: Assessing renal size, cortical thickness, echogenicity, and ruling out urinary tract obstructions.
  • Medication Reconciliation: Safely discontinuing or adjusting doses of drugs that could harm fragile kidneys, particularly non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs).

Multi-Target Protection Strategy

Dr. Aishwarya Lal designs a personalized medical protocol addressing the four pillars of renal preservation:

  1. Hemodynamic Protection: Utilizing RAS inhibitors (ACEi/ARBs) and modern SGLT2 inhibitors to relieve intraglomerular pressure and significantly reduce protein loss.
  2. Metabolic & Glycemic Optimization: Tight yet safe glycemic targets without inducing hypoglycemic episodes.
  3. Cardiovascular Risk Reduction: Strict blood pressure control (<130/80 mmHg), lipid management, and volume moderation.
  4. Nutritional Counseling: Calibrated protein intake, customized sodium restriction (<2g sodium daily), and potassium guidance aligned with your blood levels.
When should you consult Dr. Aishwarya Lal?

If your routine health checkup reveals elevated serum creatinine (>1.2 mg/dL for women, >1.4 mg/dL for men), protein in your urine, persistent morning swelling in feet or face, or if you have lived with diabetes or hypertension for more than 5 years.
