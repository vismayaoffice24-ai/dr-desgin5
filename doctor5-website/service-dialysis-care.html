Dialysis Care, Planning & Management

Expert medical oversight for hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, vascular access preservation, and quality of life.

Compassionate, Evidence-Based Dialysis Support

Hearing that you or a loved one requires dialysis is often emotional and overwhelming. Dr. Aishwarya Lal approaches dialysis with warmth, clarity, and deep clinical experience. Dialysis is not the end of a journey—it is an effective, life-sustaining medical bridge that restores energy, removes harmful toxins and fluid, and allows patients to live fulfilling, active lives.

Whether preparing for elective transition to dialysis at Stage 4/5 CKD or managing chronic weekly treatments, Dr. Aishwarya provides comprehensive oversight to ensure treatments are safe, comfortable, and individualized.

Two Primary Modalities of Dialysis:

• Hemodialysis (HD): Blood is gently circulated through an artificial filter (dialyzer) in a clinic, usually 2 to 3 sessions per week (4 hours each).
• Peritoneal Dialysis (PD / CAPD): The patient's natural abdominal lining (peritoneum) acts as the filter, using sterile fluid exchanges performed conveniently at home.

Comprehensive Dialysis Services Offered

  • Timely Vascular Access Planning: Arranging early Arteriovenous (AV) Fistula creation months before dialysis starts to avoid uncomfortable emergency neck catheters.
  • AV Fistula Preservation & Care: Monitoring thrill, bruit, and blood flow rates to ensure long-term patency and prevent clotting or infections.
  • Dialysis Adequacy Audits (Kt/V & URR): Calculating clearance efficiency to guarantee that treatments are thoroughly removing metabolic wastes.
  • Renal Anemia & Iron Management: Prescribing modern erythropoietin-stimulating agents (ESAs) and intravenous iron to maintain robust hemoglobin levels and combat fatigue.
  • Mineral & Bone Disorder (MBD) Control: Balancing calcium, phosphorus, and parathyroid hormone (PTH) to prevent bone weakness and cardiovascular calcifications.
  • Peritoneal Dialysis Training & Catheter Care: Assisting patients who choose home peritoneal dialysis with catheter placement, exit-site hygiene, and peritonitis prevention.
Patient-First Philosophy:

"Dialysis should adapt to your life, not the other way around. With proper adequacy, balanced nutrition, and careful access care, our patients continue to work, travel, and spend meaningful time with their families."
